⚠️ Air Quality Protocols (PM2.5)

😷 N95 Mask Mandate

N95 masks are strictly required for transit between campus buildings. Ensure distribution points at all faculty entrances are fully stocked.

🌬️ Indoor Air Purification

Activate heavy-duty HVAC HEPA filters across all lecture halls. Keep all dormitory, classroom, and office windows completely sealed.

🌡️ Thermal Protocols (Heat Wave)

💧 Mandatory Hydration Stations

Deploy mobile water stations in high-traffic shaded areas. Send SMS alerts reminding staff and students to hydrate hourly.

👷 Campus Staff Work Limits

Restrict outdoor maintenance, landscaping, and security staff to 15-minute shifts outdoors between 11:00 AM and 4:00 PM.

🛑 Universal High-Risk Actions (Combined Exposure)

🚫 Outdoor Activities Suspended

All physical education classes and outdoor campus events are cancelled. Sports facilities must operate strictly indoors.

🚌 Increased Transit Frequency

Campus EV shuttles must operate at a 5-minute frequency to minimize outdoor walking and heat/PM2.5 exposure for all individuals.
